The two estimator calls fan out in parallel via `asyncio.gather`. Total wall time is roughly the slowest single sub, not the sum. The supplier hop runs after the estimates clear because you may want to gate it on a budget threshold.

## How to start

1. **Run the quick start.** Spin up a single TACO agent locally — your orchestrator stub. [Two minutes.](/docs/getting-started/quick-start)
2. **Wrap your platform.** Add a TACO sidecar in front of Procore, ACC, or your homegrown system so it shows up in the registry. [Sidecar guide.](/docs/getting-started/integrate-platform)
3. **Pick one workflow to automate.** Start with the chain recipe — it's the highest-leverage entry point because every other recipe extends it.
4. **Bring your subs in.** Each sub agent that adopts TACO becomes interoperable across every project, not just yours.
