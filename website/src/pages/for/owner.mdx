## Why this matters now

The construction software stack used to be one suite per role: a CAD platform, a project management platform, a financials platform. AI is changing that — every team is shopping for or building specialized agents (takeoff, estimating, RFI drafting, safety auditing, scheduling). Without a shared protocol, every one of those agents is a new integration to maintain and a new place your project data sprawls into.

TACO defines a shared vocabulary so the agents you adopt now don't become tomorrow's migration project.

## How to bring it to your projects

1. **Read [Why TACO](/docs/why-taco)** — the framing your team will share with vendors.
2. **Talk to your GC.** Ask which agents and platforms they're using. Find out which already speak TACO or could with a [sidecar](/docs/getting-started/integrate-platform).
3. **Update your next RFP.** Require typed artifact outputs and TACO-compatible discovery for any agent-based deliverable.
4. **Ask vendors for their roadmap.** The construction software stack is moving fast. Vendors that won't commit to an interoperable layer are flagging future migration cost.
