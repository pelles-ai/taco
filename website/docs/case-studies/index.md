# Case Studies

These narratives show TACO in motion across a full project workflow. They're fictional but realistic — composed from patterns we've seen in early deployments. Use them to understand how the cookbook recipes fit together in actual project flow, not as authoritative records of real projects.

## Available

- [**A typical week on the Oakridge Medical project**](./oakridge-medical-week) — *steady-state at scale*. Five days, six agents, every recipe in motion across a 50-story medical project's mechanical rough-in phase.
- [**Stafford Mechanical's first TACO deployment**](./stafford-mechanical-onboarding) — *adoption-mode at small scale*. Three weeks following a regional mechanical sub's path from "what is TACO" to discoverable by every GC on their roster.

## What case studies are for

When a doc page says "an RFI round-trip is 3 hours instead of 4 days," you have to take it on faith. When a case study walks you through Monday morning's RFI from emission at 8:12 AM to typed response at 11:47 AM with the actual JSON artifacts shown, the same claim is concrete.

These are reading materials, not tutorials. For step-by-step recipes you can run, see the [Cookbook](../cookbook/).

## Contributing a case study

If you're running TACO in production and have a workflow story worth sharing (sanitized, of course), [open a PR](https://github.com/pelles-ai/taco/edit/main/website/docs/case-studies/index.md) or get in touch via [Discussions](https://github.com/pelles-ai/taco/discussions). The format we converged on:

1. **Project setup** — scale, agents involved, trust tiers
2. **Day-by-day or hour-by-hour** narrative
3. Real (or realistic) **JSON artifacts** at key moments
4. **What the week shows** — observations about the operational shift
5. **Caveats** — what the clean narrative glosses over
